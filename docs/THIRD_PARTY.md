- Engine: Godot 4.7.2 Stable, official commit ed1daf0bf, MIT license. https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.7.2-stable/
- Godot license and bundled third-party notices: https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/LICENSE.txt and https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/COPYRIGHT.txt . The engine's complete notices are also included in exported engine binaries.
- Tower forest, architectural kit and authored fracture meshes: supplied by the project owner in `资产模型/`. Original files remain unchanged. Ownership/license rights remain with their respective owners.
- Gameplay scripts, fixed scene authoring tools, placeholder icon and synthesized impact/grapple sounds: created for this prototype. The seven 0.03 WAV layers are generated by `tools/make_sfx003.py` from deterministic waveforms/noise; no external samples.
